# Fly Free - Business Overview

**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Status:** Live & Growing  
**Business Model:** B2C E-Commerce (Custom Design T-Shirts & Apparel)

---

## What We're Achieving

Fly Free is a modern, customer-centric apparel e-commerce platform that empowers users to:

1. **Browse & Purchase Premium Apparel** - High-quality t-shirts, jerseys, polos, and oversized wear with seasonal themes
2. **Customize Designs** - Submit custom design requests for personalized apparel
3. **Share & Refer** - Influencer referral program with 10% discount codes for followers
4. **Build Collections** - Save favorite items in wishlist and track orders
5. **Fast & Secure Checkout** - Razorpay payment integration with address management

## Sales Channels

### **1. Direct E-Commerce** 🛒
- Website (web app at `/products`)
- Browse catalog
- Filter by category, color, size
- Add to cart and checkout with Razorpay

### **2. Custom Design Service** 🎨
- Submit custom designs via `/custom-design`
- Choose placement (chest, back, sleeve, full)
- Select quantity, color, size
- Admin reviews and sets price
- Customer approves and pays
- Fulfillment and delivery

### **3. Influencer Referral Program** 👥
- Influencers get dedicated 5-6 products
- Create custom discount codes (10% off)
- Followers apply code to eligible products only
- Track referral sales
- Build community & brand loyalty

### **4. Corporate Gifting** 🎁
- Hamper collections for corporations
- Bulk order support
- Custom packaging options
- Perfect for employee gifts, client appreciation

## Business Model & Revenue

### **Revenue Streams**
1. **Product Sales** - Direct apparel purchases (80% of revenue)
2. **Custom Design Orders** - Premium design service (15% of revenue)
3. **Influencer-Driven Sales** - Referral commissions (5% of revenue)
4. **Bundle Sales** - Combo deals and hampers (included in product sales)

### **Pricing Strategy**
- Regular t-shirts: ₹399-699
- Polos & jerseys: ₹599-899
- Oversized wear: ₹499-799
- Bundles: 10-20% discount
- Custom designs: Based on complexity (₹1000+)
- Influencer discount: 10% off (customer pays 90%)

### **Margins**
- Standard product: 50-60% gross margin
- Custom design: 70-80% gross margin
- Referral: 5-10% commission
